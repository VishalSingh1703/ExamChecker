What Manual Grading Actually Costs You

The cheque written to examiners is only the visible part. Four costs compound every single cycle.

⏳

Time that can't be recovered

25–40 minutes per answer sheet. Across 6 subjects that is 2.5–4 hours of skilled professional time for a single student — time taken directly out of lesson planning and teaching.

💸

Direct grading spend

At ₹15–₹20 per sheet across 5–6 subjects, each student costs ₹75–₹120 to mark. A 500-student institution spends ₹37,500–₹60,000 per cycle — before any admin overhead.

⚖️

Inconsistent marking

Fatigue, mood and interpretation drift mean the 90th script is not marked like the 1st. Two students writing the same answer can receive different scores — which invites disputes and erodes trust.

🗂️

No searchable record

Paper leaves no audit trail. Re-checking a mark, answering a parent's query or analysing which topics a class failed means physically retrieving stacks of scripts.

The hidden multiplier: slow grading delays feedback. By the time marks reach a student two or three weeks later, the class has moved on and the corrective value of the exam is largely lost.

Section 06

Accuracy You Can Defend

A grading tool is only worth adopting if you can stand behind its marks in front of a parent. ExamChecker uses the key-points method that professional examiners are trained to follow.

For each question the teacher supplies the model answer. ExamChecker breaks it into distinct scoreable points — typically one per mark — and for each point awards:

  • Full credit — the student stated the point correctly. Spelling and grammar are ignored.
  • Half credit — partially addressed: vague, incomplete, or containing a minor error.
  • No credit — wrong, reversed, or not mentioned at all.

The score is credits earned ÷ total scoreable points. The same formula works for a 1-mark definition and a 15-mark essay. Three marking strictness levels — Easy, Medium and Strict — let you match your institution's standard, and all marks round to clean 0.5 increments.

“Factual errors — swapping terms, reversing cause and effect, citing the wrong name — always reduce credit for that point. A long, verbose answer that gets the facts wrong still loses marks. A short, precise answer covering every point earns full marks. Brevity is never penalised.”
ExamChecker Marking Specification

The teacher is never removed from the loop. Extracted text and awarded marks are both editable, and any answer can be re-evaluated after correction. ExamChecker is built to do the reading and the arithmetic — not to overrule your professional judgement.
